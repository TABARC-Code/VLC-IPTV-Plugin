IPTV Channel Player v.1.6 | TAVARC-Code

Install Python 3.10+ with Tkinter and VLC. No pip dependencies.
Windows: double-click Launch Windows.bat. macOS/Linux: python3 iptv.py.
Open channels.csv in a spreadsheet and replace disabled examples with your authorised URLs.
Keep the header, use UTF-8 CSV, unique IDs and enabled=true for active channels.
Open your CSV in the app and choose Open VLC. See Help & usage inside the app.
